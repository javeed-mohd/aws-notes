DNS Records:
============

TTL -> Time to Live -> How much time to live in cache, before issuing next request or How much time to update
A -> Holds the IP address of your domain

High TTL -> DNS overload increases, stable websites will have high TTL
Low TTL  -> Migration activities, decrease TTL

3600S (1 Hour)

at least 1 day before reduce TTL to 1sec

once everything is fine, take TTL back to 3600
