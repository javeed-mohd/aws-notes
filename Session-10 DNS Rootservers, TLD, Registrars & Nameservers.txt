Root servers (RS) ==> 13 root servers across the world managed by different organisations and governments to control TLD's and part of ICANN. DNS resolutions requests from ISP comes to root servers.

Top Level Domains (TLD) ==> Top level domains, basically who sells the domains. It has the complete details of the domains booked, available, buyer information, etc.,

Registrars ==> Who sells the domains, basically mediators between TLD and end users. Who can manage the domain too	

Nameservers (NS) ==> Nameservers, who is currently managing the domain, we can buy domain with one registrar and we can transfer the management to another registrar by updating NS records.
